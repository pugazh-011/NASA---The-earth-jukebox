    03 / SCIENCE & TRANSPARENCY

    Science first.
    Sound as another lens.

    El Niño is the warm phase of the El Niño–Southern Oscillation (ENSO), a coupled ocean–atmosphere pattern in the tropical Pacific. The quantity shown here is a sea-surface-height–based indicator derived from satellite altimetry. It is not a sea-surface-temperature map, and the chart does not label individual dates as El Niño or La Niña.
